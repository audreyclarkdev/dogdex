Capstone concept for March 2026 students

The dog-spotting app that turns every walk into a collectible adventure.

DogDex is a mobile-first web application for dog lovers who want to discover, track, and learn about breeds they encounter in real life. Part field guide, part journal, part collection game.

Explore the gallery See the feature set

Project snapshot

  • Browse a full encyclopedia of recognized dog breeds
  • Mark breeds as spotted in the wild
  • Log photos, locations, dates, and notes
  • Return daily for a fresh Breed of the Day

Inspired by bird-watching and the classic Pokédex loop, DogDex turns dog encounters into moments of discovery. The goal is simple: notice more, learn more, and build a personal collection of real-world sightings.

Core experience

Built for curious dog people, neighborhood explorers, and collectors.

01

Searchable breed encyclopedia

Browse a complete list of recognized breeds with names, photos, origin country, and standout traits.

02

Spot and collect

Check off breeds you have encountered and build a visible record of your growing dog knowledge.

03

Detailed breed profiles

Open a breed page to learn lifespan, weight range, energy level, temperament, and origin details.

04

Personal sighting log

Attach a photo, location, date, and notes so each sighting becomes a memory instead of a vague "I think I saw one."

05

Breed of the Day

Surface one breed every morning to give the app a reason to be opened daily and make learning feel lightweight.

06

Progress filters

Toggle between all breeds, spotted breeds, and the ones you are still hoping to find.

Daily loop

How DogDex turns an ordinary walk into repeatable product behavior.

1

Spot a dog

Notice a breed on the sidewalk, at the park, or during a trip.

2

Confirm the breed

Use the app to compare photos, traits, and breed details.

3

Log the sighting

Save the moment with notes, a date, a location, and a photo.

4

Come back tomorrow
